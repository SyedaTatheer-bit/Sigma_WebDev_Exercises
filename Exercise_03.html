🌌 Space smells like burnt steak Astronauts returning from spacewalks have reported that their suits carry a smell similar to seared steak or hot metal, caused by high-energy vibrations of atoms in space.

🦋 Butterflies taste with their feet They have special sensors on their feet that help them detect whether the surface they land on is suitable for laying eggs or feeding.

🌍 Bananas are berries, but strawberries aren’t Botanically speaking, bananas qualify as berries, while strawberries do not because of how their seeds are arranged..

🦑 Octopuses have three hearts Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body. Interestingly, the main heart stops beating when they swim.

🔥 The hottest chili pepper can cause hallucinations The Carolina Reaper, one of the world’s spiciest peppers, is so intense that eating it can trigger endorphin overload, sometimes leading to hallucinations.
